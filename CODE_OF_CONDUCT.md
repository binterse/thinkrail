# Code of Conduct

This policy applies to participation in the unofficial `binterse/thinkrail` fork. Its maintainer is [Björn (@binterse)](https://github.com/binterse), independently of JetBrains.

## Expected behavior

Treat people with respect. Give constructive feedback, welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience, and discuss ideas without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, and sharing someone’s private information without permission are not acceptable.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close or restrict discussions, or block participants who violate this policy. Responses should reflect the severity and context of the behavior.

## Reporting a problem

Contact the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/binterse) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/wallback/). GitHub profiles do not offer private messaging. For a sensitive report, use LinkedIn messaging where available; do not put private details in a public discussion or issue.

The fork’s GitHub Issues tracker is currently disabled. If enabled later, issues are public and should not be used for confidential conduct reports. Share only the information needed to explain the problem, and avoid posting other people’s personal information.

## Upstream community

Participation in JetBrains’ ThinkRail community is governed separately by the [upstream Code of Conduct](https://github.com/JetBrains/thinkrail/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Its reporting channels apply to that community; JetBrains does not administer conduct reports for this personal fork.
